台灣規則 How to Play

  • 雙方輪流在空的交叉點落子，黑先。
    Take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Black goes first.
  • 棋串旁邊的空點是它的氣；沒有氣的棋子會被提走。
    Empty points next to a group are its liberties. A group with none is captured.
  • 打劫：單子提劫後，對方不能立刻提回，必須先在別處下一手。
    Ko: you can't retake a single stone immediately — play elsewhere first.
  • 禁著點：不能下讓自己的棋沒有氣的位置（除非能提掉對方的子）。
    No suicide: you can't play where your stones would have no liberties, unless it captures.
  • 雙方都虛手後終局，標出死子再計算。
    When both players pass, mark dead stones and count.
  • 數子法：活棋子數 + 圍住的空點，單官雙方各得一半。黑棋貼 7.5 目（= 3¾ 子），所以 19 路黑 185 子勝、13 路 89 子勝、9 路 45 子勝。
    Area counting: stones + surrounded points, neutral points split. Black gives 7.5 komi, so Black needs 185 on 19×19 (89 on 13×13, 45 on 9×9).
